About ReVogue

A capstone project by Yap Zhi Xuan studying at Sunway University, BSc Software Engineering

ReVogue exists to bridge the gap between what technology can do and what people genuinely need from their wardrobe.

The fashion industry contributes up to 10% of global carbon emissions, yet most people wear only a fraction of the clothes they own. ReVogue is built on a simple belief: the most sustainable wardrobe is the one you already have.

Using AI-powered clothing recognition, mood-based outfit recommendations, and colour psychology tools, ReVogue helps you rediscover and reuse what you own, reducing decision fatigue and encouraging conscious consumption aligned with UN SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.

Every recommendation comes with a plain-language explanation. ReVogue is designed to be transparent, inclusive, and genuinely useful, not just technically impressive.
